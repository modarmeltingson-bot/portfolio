Running the auction side of a real estate brokerage

As Auction Manager, I keep every deal, listing and lead moving: coordinating transactions from contract to close, keeping the website and MLS listings accurate, and running the CRM and automations behind the sales team.

  • Transaction coordination: buyer intake, purchase price, earnest money, marketing fee and closing date tracked for every deal

Alley Realty & Auction, Landing Page Sellers Campaign

A seller landing page that turns visitors into auction listings

Homeowners thinking about a quick cash sale often lose money to wholesalers. I built a campaign that shows them a better option: auction the home, and start with a free valuation.

  • Landing page built in WordPress and Elementor, with a free valuation form front and center

Lean On Me Home Care, Skye Care app

Skye Care: the app I created to run Lean On Me's office

Skye Care is the app and login tracker I created for Lean On Me Home Care. Caregivers log their shifts, and the office manages clients, schedules, payroll and invoices in one place. I built it around how the agency actually works.

  • Caregiver login tracking for every shift, including overnight 24-hour live-in shifts
  • Client and caregiver records with scheduling
  • Payroll and overtime calculated from scheduled hours, minus breaks
  • Invoices generated by date range and client, reviewed before sending
  • Admin messaging with online status, plus email and text alerts to caregivers
